# TransitPRO – Required Booking Details

Trip bookings require: full name, gender, valid Sri Lankan 10-digit phone number, travel date, at least one seat, and pickup location.

Rental bookings require: full name, valid phone number, rental date, passenger count within vehicle capacity, and pickup location.

The frontend disables confirmation until required fields are complete, and the backend validates the same requirements so they cannot be bypassed. Travel dates cannot be in the past.
